# Results Interpretation

## Topic

The mediating role of negative affect (NA) and the moderating role of positive affect (PA) in the relationship between global executive composite (GEC) and perceived stress


---
## 1. Research questions
In this mini-project, I proposed the following research questions:
### Direct Associations
- Does GEC associate with perceived stress?
- Does GEC associate with NA?
- Does NA associate with perceived stress?
- Does PA associate with GEC?
- Does PA associate with NA?

### Mediation
- Does NA mediate the relationship between GEC and perceived stress?

### Moderated Mediation
- Does PA moderate the indirect effect of GEC on perceived stress via NA?
